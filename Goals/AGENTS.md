# Goals

Goal tracker. Goals are loftier than projects — may not have concrete plans.

**Statuses:** `active` → `achieved` (also `dream` for someday goals)
**Properties:** target-date, area, milestones table.

**Hierarchy:** Tasks live in Projects, Projects push toward Goals. Goals may have multiple projects underneath.

**Agent rules:** Read for context on human's priorities. Don't create or modify goals without being asked.

**Templates:** `_admin/templates/para/Goal.md`
