# Inbox

Quick capture zone. Everything lands here first before being processed into PARA.

**What belongs here:** Unprocessed tasks, notes, ideas, web clips — anything the human dumps quickly.

**Processing rules:** During weekly review, each item gets filed to Projects, Areas, Resources, or Archive. Items leave the inbox when they gain a project, tag, or area association.

**Agent rules:** Read-only unless the human asks you to process the inbox. Never delete items — file them to the correct PARA folder.
