# Areas

Ongoing responsibilities with standards to maintain. Areas never "finish."

**Test:** If neglected for a month, do things fall apart? → Area. Just interesting? → Resource.

**Examples:** Health, Finances, Career, Home, a specific business.

**Structure:** Each area has an index page with Dataview queries for related projects, notes, goals, and people.

**Ongoing projects:** Each area may have an "ongoing" project for recurring tasks that don't fit a specific project.

**Resources can become Areas:** When a casual interest becomes a real responsibility, promote it.

**Agent rules:** Read for context on the human's life areas. Don't modify without being asked.

**Templates:** `_admin/templates/para/Area.md`
