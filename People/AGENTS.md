# People

Personal CRM. Contact records with relationship tracking.

**Properties:** name, company, title, location, phone, email, birthday, relationship type, last check-in date, areas, interests.

**Features:** Meeting notes linked to contacts, gift ideas, birthday calendar, pipeline view for sales/outreach.

**Agent rules:** Read for context when the human asks about contacts or relationships. Don't create or modify contact records without being asked. Sensitive personal data lives here — treat with care.

**Templates:** `_admin/templates/para/Person.md`
